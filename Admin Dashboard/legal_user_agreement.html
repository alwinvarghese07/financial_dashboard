User Agreement

End-user license terms, platform acceptable use commitments, and enterprise partner master agreements.

Version: 2.5  |  Effective Date: October 01, 2026
Published & Enforced

1. Account Registration & Electronic Communications

By creating an account or completing a guest booking on StayTransit, you agree to receive essential operational notifications via SMS text and email regarding booking status, flight delays, chauffeur assignments, and payment receipts.

2. Acceptable Platform Use

You agree not to exploit the platform for unauthorized automated scraping, fraudulent payment testing, or deceptive reservation requests. Any violation will result in immediate profile suspension and reporting to relevant financial enforcement bodies.

Published & Enforced

1. Master Enterprise Agreement

This Agreement binds partner agencies, corporate clients, and affiliate booking networks to reciprocal confidentiality, agreed commercial commission tiers, and intellectual property protections for the duration of the operational partnership.

2. Indemnification & Dispute Resolution

Each party agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other party from any third-party claims arising out of gross negligence or material breach of this Agreement. Any unresolved disputes shall be submitted to confidential arbitration in the State of California.
